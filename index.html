ArriveO'Clock

Sign in to sync your saved places, recent trips and alarm across devices — or skip it and use the app right away.

No account needed. Without sign-in your data stays on this device only. We use your location solely to compute live ETA.

Step 1 of 3

Set your stop.

Tap the search bar and pick where you're getting off — search any place, choose a saved one, or drop a pin on the map.

Step 2 of 3

Pick your lead time.

Choose how many minutes before arrival to wake you, plus an alarm tone. The ETA recalculates live as you move — no fixed countdown.

Step 3 of 3

Start, then relax.

Tap start and put your phone away. We track your real movement and ring just before your stop — even with the screen locked.
