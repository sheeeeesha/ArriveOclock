ArriveO'Clock

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 June 2026

ArriveO'Clock is a travel-companion app that wakes you up just before your stop, using your live location to estimate your arrival time. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Location data

With your permission, ArriveO'Clock uses your device's location to:

  • show your position on the map and detect where you are starting from;
  • continuously estimate your arrival time from your real movement and ring the alarm just before your stop.

Background location. On the mobile app, while a trip alarm is active you can grant "Allow all the time" so tracking continues with the screen off or the app in the background. This is used only to compute your live ETA and trigger the arrival alarm during an active trip. You can revoke it any time in your device settings; the alarm then only works while the app is open.

Your location is processed on your device for the alarm itself. To draw maps, search places and compute routes, location data is sent to the third-party services listed below.

Third-party services

  • OpenFreeMap — map tiles.
  • Photon (Komoot) — place search / geocoding.
  • OSRM — route geometry and distance.
  • Transitous — public-transit data, where available.
  • Supabase — secure storage for your account data when you sign in (below).

These services receive only the coordinates or query needed to perform the request. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Account & synced data

Sign-in is optional. If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email and profile photo to create your account, and we store your saved places, recent trips, alarm preferences and any rating/feedback you submit so they sync across your devices (via Supabase). If you use the app as a guest, this data stays on your device only.

If you join the mobile-app waitlist, we store the email you provide solely to notify you about the launch.

Permissions we request

  • Location (incl. background) — live ETA and the arrival alarm.
  • Notifications — to show and sound the alarm.
  • Alarms & reminders / full-screen alarm — to ring on time over the lock screen.

Data retention & deletion

Guest data lives only on your device and is removed when you clear app data or uninstall. For signed-in accounts, you can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below. Signing out removes locally cached data on that device.

Children

ArriveO'Clock is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
